01 · Opportunity

The brief after the brief

A hackathon creates products.
A campaign creates users.

After the AI Factory finale, Silpo has three strong winning solutions. Yet to a customer, they are still unfamiliar names and an unfamiliar way to shop.

My challenge is not just to announce the winners, but to guide each customer to a first moment of value: a ready cart, a saved routine, or a planned gathering.

Speculative concept. The products and hackathon results are fictional. Their scenarios are grounded in the official Silpo MCP capabilities.

02 · Research

Grounded, not sci-fi

Three patterns already
working in grocery AI.

I checked the concept against Silpo’s official documentation and current product patterns from Instacart, Shopify, and Swiggy. The strongest cases do not “talk about AI” — they close a specific routine from intent to cart.

A

Meal → cart

Meal planning and an automatic shopping list deliver an immediate wow moment with almost no learning curve.
